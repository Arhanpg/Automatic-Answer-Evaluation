# Phase 3: Updated Literature Review

The repository has not yet established an implementation gap. Closely related
work already identifies:

- Structure-aware mathematical handwriting transcription
- Over-correction of student mathematical mistakes
- Vision-capable grading of handwritten mathematics
- Large-scale handwritten university mathematics grading
- Human verification and selective review

No novelty claim is made until a targeted page/region benchmark, a
symbol-aware metric, and a grading-propagation study are completed.

Papers and venues mentioned in the research specification must be checked
against official proceedings pages and arXiv metadata before inclusion in a
manuscript. Links alone are not sufficient provenance.
