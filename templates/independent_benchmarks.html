🔬 Four-Stage Preprocessing Pipeline

Applied prior to OCR & VLM inference to eliminate ink occlusion, gridline skew, scan specks, and low contrast.

4 STAGES VERIFIED
0. Original Scan
Raw 300 DPI scan with mixed blue student ink and red examiner marks.
1a. Red Ink Separation
Dual-band HSV masking removes red pen strokes; inpaints to clean white.
1b. Teacher Marks Layer
Isolated examiner marks layer on white page without student text interference.
2. Grayscale & Deskew
Morphological gridline suppression followed by minAreaRect rotation.
3. NLM Denoising (h=10)
Eliminates paper texture artifacts while preserving minus signs and dots.
4. Sauvola Binarization
Local adaptive thresholding (w=25, k=0.2) delivering crisp binary stroke contrast.
