# Milestone M9: QLoRA and Training-Volume Ablation

## Configuration

The intended configuration is Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct with NF4 4-bit
quantization, LoRA `r=16`, `alpha=32`, batch size 1, gradient accumulation 8,
gradient checkpointing, and cosine scheduling.

## Training-volume results

| Condition | Training scripts | Training examples | MAE | RMSE | QWK | Within 1 mark |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 scripts | 5 | 20 | 1.62 | 2.14 | 0.742 | 62.5% |
| 15 scripts | 15 | 60 | 1.18 | 1.58 | 0.835 | 79.2% |
| 25 scripts | 25 | 100 | 0.94 | 1.27 | 0.884 | 87.5% |
| 25 scripts + synthetic | 25 | 150 | 0.82 | 1.12 | 0.906 | 91.7% |

## Improvement finding

Increasing training material from 5 to 25 scripts reduces MAE by 0.68 marks
and raises QWK from 0.742 to 0.884. Adding 50 synthetic OCR-degraded
examples reduces MAE by another 0.12 marks and raises QWK to 0.906.

These are deterministic/simulated ablation outputs generated by
`backend/qlora_trainer.py`; they are not evidence that live CUDA training was
completed for each condition.

## VRAM evidence

The hardware registry records CPU execution and `peak_vram_mb=0.0`. It does
not record a live RTX 3050 run. Older text that claimed 3540.2 MB actual
allocation is superseded. The 4096 MB limit and 3800 MB ceiling are
configuration values, not measured hardware usage.
