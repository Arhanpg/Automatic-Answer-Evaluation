## 1. Evidence Hierarchy

Every claim, metric, table cell, and performance report in Paper 2 must be backed by verifiable data classified into one of the following official tiers:

1. **`VERIFIED_LIVE`**:
   - The result was generated by executing live code during the current research phase on local hardware.
   - Per-sample predictions, execution logs, run configuration, environment metadata, and timestamps are persisted on disk.
2. **`VERIFIED_EXISTING_ARTIFACT`**:
   - The result relies on an established, audited disk artifact from an earlier verified phase (e.g., Phase 9 live OCR benchmark predictions).
   - The file's provenance, checksum, and generation script have been independently verified.
3. **`PROVISIONAL`**:
   - Exploratory results where data collection or rater consensus is still active. Must not be used for final publication claims without explicit caveats.
4. **`EXPLORATORY`**:
   - Secondary descriptive observations on small subgroups (e.g., historical 6-student split) where statistical power is limited.
5. **`SYNTHETIC`**:
   - Controlled perturbations or synthetic augmentations used strictly for training-time robustness or stress testing.
   - **MANDATORY**: Synthetic records can NEVER be included in test evaluation sets or reported as authentic student observations.
6. **`NOT_RUN` / `FAILED` / `UNAVAILABLE`**:
   - A model, library, service, or hardware configuration that was not executed due to documented blockers (e.g. `bitsandbytes` missing on Windows).

## 2. Anti-Leakage Invariants

To guarantee scientific validity, the pipeline enforces five strict anti-leakage invariants verified by automated tests:

- **Invariant 1 (Strict Group Isolation)**: No student ID may ever appear in both the training and evaluation split of any fold.
- **Invariant 2 (Strict Target Blinding)**: No feature extractor, vectorizer, scaler, prompt builder, or retriever may receive `y_test`, teacher marks, or derived residual errors.
- **Invariant 3 (Target Invariance Test)**: Permuting or altering `y_test` after inference MUST produce zero change in saved predictions.
- **Invariant 4 (Fit-on-Train-Only)**: TF-IDF vectorizers, BM25 corpus dictionaries, StandardScaler means/variances, and calibration weights must be fitted exclusively on training-fold data.
- **Invariant 5 (Retrieval Candidate Exclusion)**: RAG exemplars must be sourced exclusively from training-fold students, strictly excluding the query student and test-fold students.
